HOME WORKOUT

A Beginner-Friendly Home Workout

4 min read • FitLife

When you want to move at home, bodyweight exercises can be a convenient starting point. Use a clear space and focus on controlled movement.

Warm-up

Spend a few minutes with gentle marching, arm circles and easy movement.

Workout ideas

  • Bodyweight squats
  • Wall or incline push-ups
  • Glute bridges
  • Standing knee raises
  • Gentle mobility exercises

Finish comfortably

Slow down gradually and include gentle stretching if it feels comfortable. Adjust exercises to your ability and take breaks when needed.
